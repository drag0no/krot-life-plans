* **Property Budget Match:** A modern 3-bed home (€350k–€420k + 5% VAT) consumes only ~35%–39% of net worth, leaving €600k–€700k active capital deployed.
* **The Play:** Settle under Category 6.2 Lifetime PR, manage capital at 0% tax, and travel globally using Brazilian passports, completely bypassing Cypriot language exams and compulsory military service.

### 2. Runner-Up — Portugal (Overall Score: 8.8 / 10)
* **Why It Wins:** The most dependable route to full **European Union citizenship**. Holding Brazilian passports statutorily **waives the Portuguese language exam** (*Estatuto de Igualdade de Direitos e Deveres*).
* **The 2026 Nationality Reform:** Under *Lei Orgânica n.º 1/2026*, the citizenship timeline for CPLP nationals is **7 years** (counted from first card issue date), while **Permanent Residency (Article 80) remains accessible at Year 5**.
* **Property Budget Match:** High-spec 3-bed apartments in Matosinhos Sul (Porto) or Parque das Nações (Lisbon) cost €380,000–€480,000 (~35%–42% of NW).
* **Trade-off:** Realized trading gains face a flat 28% capital gains tax (D7 passive receipts are 100% exempt from Portuguese Social Security).

### 3. Tech Alternative — United Kingdom (Overall Score: 7.2 / 10)
* **Why It Appeals:** Pavel qualifies for the **Global Talent Visa (Digital Technology)** endorsed by Tech Nation without employer sponsorship or a job offer. Fast-track Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) is accessible in **3 years** for Exceptional Talent, and British citizenship 12 months later (Year 5).
* **Critical Distinction:** Naturalization grants a **British Passport** (full rights in the UK and Ireland Common Travel Area), but **does NOT grant European Union freedom of movement** post-Brexit.
* **The Property Constraint:** Buying a 3-bed home in London (£650k–£900k) consumes 65%–90% of net worth, directly violating the 50% cap. To stay compliant, the family must either rent (£2,800–£3,500/mo) or purchase in commuter tech corridors (Reading, outer Cambridge, Manchester MediaCity) at £420k–£490k.
* **Tax Considerations:** The 4-year Foreign Income & Gains (FIG) regime provides a 4-year tax-free window on foreign income/gains, but Year 5+ exposes capital to 24% CGT or up to 45% Income Tax.

### 4. The Spanish 2-Year Naturalization Trap (Strict Warning)
* Spain's celebrated 2-year naturalization concession applies strictly to *naturales* (citizens by origin born in Ibero-American countries).
* Because Pavel and Anna were born in Russia, acquiring Brazilian citizenship makes them **naturalized Brazilians**. Spanish civil registries classify them under Spain's standard **10-year residency rule**, exposing them to Spain's aggressive worldwide taxation and annual Wealth Tax. Only their Brazilian-born child would qualify in 2 years.
