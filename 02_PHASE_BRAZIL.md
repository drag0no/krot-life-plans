## 3. CHILD BIRTHRIGHT CITIZENSHIP & PASSPORT (MONTH 3)

### 3.1 Unconditional *Jus Soli* Constitutionality
Under **Article 12, I, "a" of the Constitution of the Federative Republic of Brazil**, any child born on Brazilian soil is automatically a Brazilian citizen by origin (*brasileiro nato*), regardless of the parents' nationality or immigration status.

### 3.2 Registration & Passport Issuance Procedure:
1. **Medical Birth Statement (DNV):** Upon delivery, the hospital issues the yellow triplicate *Declaração de Nascido Vivo* (DNV) signed by the delivering physician.
2. **Civil Registry Registration (*Cartório*):** Present the DNV, parents' passports, and marriage certificate at the local *Cartório de Registro Civil das Pessoas Naturais*. The civil registrar immediately issues the official Brazilian birth certificate (*Certidão de Nascimento de Inteiro Teor*) free of charge within 24 to 48 hours.
3. **Brazilian Biometric Passport Issuance:**
   * Book an appointment online via the Polícia Federal passport scheduling portal.
   * Attend the appointment with the child, birth certificate, parent IDs, and biometric photo.
   * The biometric passport is manufactured and delivered within **5 to 10 business days**.
   * Strategic Benefit: The child is an EU-visa-free Brazilian citizen from birth, with unrestricted right of abode across all of South America.

## 5. THE 1-YEAR EXPEDITED NATURALIZATION CLOCK (MONTHS 4–15)

Standard foreign naturalization in Brazil requires 4 years of continuous residence. Having a Brazilian child slashes this requirement to **1 single year**:

### 5.1 Legal Statute: Lei nº 13.445/2017, Article 65, II, "a"
Foreign nationals who have a Brazilian child qualify for naturalization (*Naturalização Ordinária*) after **1 year of continuous legal residence** in Brazil, provided they demonstrate:
1. Civil capacity under Brazilian law.
2. Clean criminal records in Brazil and abroad.
3. Demonstrated ability to communicate in Portuguese.

### 5.2 Physical Presence Invariant: The 90-Day Travel Ceiling
* To satisfy the statutory definition of "continuous residence" (*residência contínua*), Pavel and Anna must maintain Brazil as their habitual principal residence.

### 5.3 Portuguese Language Certification: Celpe-Bras Exam

* **Exam Structure:**
