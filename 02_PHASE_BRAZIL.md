## 3. CHILD BIRTHRIGHT CITIZENSHIP & PASSPORT (MONTH 3)

### 3.1 Unconditional *Jus Soli* Constitutionality
Under **Article 12, I, "a" of the Constitution of the Federative Republic of Brazil**, any child born on Brazilian soil is automatically a Brazilian citizen by origin (*brasileiro nato*), regardless of the parents' nationality or immigration status.

### 3.2 Registration & Passport Issuance Procedure:
1. **Medical Birth Statement (DNV):** Upon delivery, the hospital issues the yellow triplicate *Declaração de Nascido Vivo* (DNV) signed by the delivering physician.
2. **Civil Registry Registration (*Cartório*):** Present the DNV, parents' passports, and marriage certificate at the local *Cartório de Registro Civil das Pessoas Naturais*. The civil registrar immediately issues the official Brazilian birth certificate (*Certidão de Nascimento de Inteiro Teor*) free of charge within 24 to 48 hours.
3. **Brazilian Biometric Passport Issuance:**
   * Book an appointment online via the Polícia Federal passport scheduling portal.
   * Attend the appointment with the child, birth certificate, parent IDs, and biometric photo.
   * The biometric passport is manufactured and delivered within **5 to 10 business days**.
   * Strategic Benefit: The child is an EU-visa-free Brazilian citizen from birth, with unrestricted right of abode across all of South America.


## 4. PARENTAL PERMANENT RESIDENCY VIA FAMILY REUNIFICATION (MONTHS 3–4)

Once the child's birth certificate is issued, both Pavel and Anna hold the statutory right to permanent residency based on family reunion with a Brazilian child.

### 4.1 Legal Basis
* **Normative Resolution:** *Portaria Interministerial nº 12/2018* and *Lei nº 13.445/2017* (Article 37).
* **Status Granted:** Indefinite Permanent Residence (*Autorização de Residência por Reunião Familiar*).

### 4.2 Document Checklist for Polícia Federal:
1. **Child's Documentation:** Original Brazilian birth certificate (*Certidão de Nascimento de Inteiro Teor*).
2. **Passports:** Valid Russian passports.
3. **Apostilled Marriage Certificate:** Russian marriage certificate, apostilled in Russia and translated in Brazil by an official sworn public translator (*tradutor juramentado*).
4. **Apostilled Criminal Clearances:** Russian federal criminal history certificate (*Spravka o nesudimosti*), apostilled and sworn-translated.
5. **Local Police Clearance:** Brazilian state and federal criminal background checks (generated online instantly via Polícia Federal and Polícia Civil portals).
6. **Proof of Financial Means & Residence:** Bank statements demonstrating liquid capital (Interactive Brokers / cash) and residential lease agreement registered with a local *Cartório*.

### 4.3 Issuance of Migration Registration Cards (CRNM)
* Processing time: 30 to 60 days following submission at the Federal Police station.
* Documents Issued: Digital and physical *Carteira de Registro Nacional Migratório* (CRNM) cards with permanent status.
