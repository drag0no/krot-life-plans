### 3.2 The Core Compliance Risks of Lowering Salary
While saving ~SGD 5k–7k prior to Q3 2027 departure is attractive, reducing salary during EP renewal introduces severe asymmetric risks:
1. **Automated Salary-Reduction Audit Flag:** MOM’s automated work pass processing algorithms flag renewal applications where an applicant’s declared salary drops substantially (e.g. >15%–30%) while remaining in the same job title (Managing Director).
2. **Substance & "Shell Company" Scrutiny:** When an owner-operated micro-firm reduces its director’s remuneration, MOM frequently issues a formal Request for Information (RFI) requiring management accounts, P&L statements, and commercial contracts. Because Fedot Capital produces no external client revenue, an in-depth MOM manual audit threatens pass approval.
3. **Stage 1 Age-Benchmark Floor:** In Financial Services, the mandatory Stage 1 minimum qualifying salary rises with age to ~SGD 9,500–10,500/month for applicants in their late 30s. Dropping salary below the sectoral age threshold results in immediate disqualification.
4. **Asymmetric Risk/Reward:** Risking non-renewal to save ~SGD 4,700–7,400 on a net worth of ~SGD 1,700,000 (<0.45% of net worth) represents poor risk-adjusted judgment.
