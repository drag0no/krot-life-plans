## 2. DEEP-DIVE ANALYSIS OF SPECIALIZED DIMENSIONS

### 2.1 Ability to Have Multiple Passports (Legal Capacity)

Holding multiple citizenships is essential for your family's sovereign mobility and risk diversification:

* **Singapore (Strictly 1 Passport):** Under Article 126 of the Constitution of the Republic of Singapore, taking Singaporean citizenship requires the mandatory renunciation of all foreign citizenships. Dual citizenship is strictly forbidden for adults.
* **Brazil (Unlimited Multiple Passports):** Under **Constitutional Amendment EC 131/2023**, Brazilian citizens are constitutionally protected when acquiring foreign citizenships without losing Brazilian nationality. Furthermore, foreign nationals naturalizing in Brazil (under Art. 65 of Lei 13.445/2017) are **never required to renounce their Russian citizenship**.
* **Hungary (Unlimited Multiple Passports):** The Hungarian Citizenship Act allows dual and multiple citizenships unconditionally.
* **Portugal (Unlimited Multiple Passports):** Under the 1981 *Lei da Nacionalidade*, Portugal fully permits dual and multiple citizenships without restriction.
* **Cyprus (Unlimited Multiple Passports):** The Civil Registry Law of Cyprus unconditionally permits dual and multiple citizenships. Naturalizing in Cyprus does not require dropping Russian or Brazilian nationality.
* **Belgium (Unlimited Multiple Passports):** Under the Belgian Nationality Code, Belgium has unconditionally permitted dual and multiple citizenship since 2008 without requiring the renunciation of prior nationalities.
* **United Kingdom (Unlimited Multiple Passports):** Section 6 of the British Nationality Act 1981 permits multiple nationalities. Acquiring British citizenship does not impact Russian or Brazilian passports.
* **Poland (Unlimited Multiple Passports):** Under the Polish Citizenship Act of 2009, Polish law fully permits dual and multiple citizenship. Polish citizens may hold foreign passports without legal encumbrance.
