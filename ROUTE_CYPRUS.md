* Under Category 6.2 PR regulations, there is **NO strict daily physical presence requirement** to maintain your permanent residency.

### 3.3 The 17-Year Cyprus Non-Domicile (Non-Dom) Tax Sanctuary
Once resident in Cyprus, you register under the **Cyprus Non-Domicile Regime** (applicable to foreign nationals who were not tax residents of Cyprus for 17 of the past 20 years):
1. **0% Capital Gains Tax on Trading:**
   * Profits from the sale of securities (shares, bonds, foreign exchange, options, futures, and other financial derivative instruments) are **100% exempt from Cyprus income and capital gains tax**.
   * Only capital gains from real estate situated physically inside Cyprus are taxed (at 20%).
2. **0% Tax on Dividends:**
   * Dividends paid into your personal accounts (from foreign companies or local holding structures) are **100% exempt from the Special Defence Contribution (SDC)** (which normally taxes local domiciles at 17%).
3. **0% Tax on Interest Income:**
   * Completely exempt from SDC (normally 17% or 30%).
4. **General Healthcare System (GeSY) Contribution:**
   * Passive income (dividends/interest) and trading profits are subject to a **2.65% GeSY healthcare contribution**, which is legally capped at a maximum annual income ceiling of **€180,000**.
   * **Maximum Annual Tax Drag:** Capped at exactly **€4,770/year**, regardless of how many hundreds of thousands of euros you generate in trading profits.
5. **Tax Residency Options:**
   * *183-Day Rule:* Spend >183 days per calendar year in Cyprus.
   * *60-Day Rule:* Spend at least 60 days in Cyprus, spend less than 183 days in any other single country, maintain a permanent residential home (your owned property), and carry out business/directorship or reside as an independent economic actor.
