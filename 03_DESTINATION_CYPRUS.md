## 2. FAST-TRACK CATEGORY 6.2 PR CRITERIA & REAL ESTATE RULES

Regulation 6(2) of the Aliens and Immigration Regulations provides the fastest, most secure route to permanent European residency:

### 2.1 Real Estate Investment Parameters
* **Contract Value:** Minimum **€300,000 + VAT** directly from a land development company.
* **New-Build Mandate:** The dwelling must be a **first-time sale** directly from a developer. Resale properties are strictly disqualified by law.
* **Upfront Payment Rule:** Proof that at least **€200,000 + VAT** has been paid to the developer's bank account before lodging the application.
* **Strict Foreign Wire Trail:** Funds must originate from foreign banking accounts (Singapore / Brazil) directly into Cyprus via SWIFT wire transfers. Bank transfer confirmations proving foreign origin are legally mandatory.

### 2.2 Reduced 5% VAT Regulations on Primary Residences
Under revised Cypriot VAT legislation:
* A **reduced 5% VAT rate** applies to the first **130 square meters** of buildable area for a primary family residence, up to a maximum property value of **€350,000**.
* To qualify for the reduced rate:
  * The total buildable area of the property must not exceed **190 square meters**.
  * The total transaction value must not exceed **€475,000**.
* Any excess value or area above the €350,000 / 130 m² threshold (up to the €475,000 / 190 m² ceiling) is taxed at the standard 19% VAT rate.
* Properties exceeding €475,000 or 190 m² are disqualified from the 5% reduction and taxed entirely at the standard 19% rate.

### 2.3 Fulfilling the €75,000 Annual Foreign Income Mandate
The Civil Registry and Migration Department (CRMD) enforces specific foreign passive income minimums for Category 6.2:
* Main Applicant (Pavel): **€50,000 / year**
* Spouse (Anna): **€15,000 / year**
* Minor Child: **€10,000 / year**
* **Total Statutory Floor (Family of 3):** **€75,000 / year** (~$82,500 USD).
* **Documentary Proof:** Category 6.2 strictly requires foreign income to be verified via an **official tax return from the country of tax residency**. The certified Brazilian DIRPF tax return and DARF payment voucher generated in [`02_PHASE_BRAZIL.md`](02_PHASE_BRAZIL.md) (showing $89k–$99k USD realized gains taxed at 15%) satisfies this requirement without question.

### 2.4 Abolition of Annual Income Re-Verification
* **Regulatory Modernization:** The Cyprus Migration Department has **officially abolished** the requirement for Category 6.2 permit holders to submit annual proof of foreign income.
* **Remaining Ongoing Obligations:**
  1. Maintain continuous ownership of the qualifying real estate (or replace it with an equivalent qualifying property).
  2. Maintain active private health insurance for all family members.
  3. Submit a clean criminal record clearance certificate every **three years** (instead of annually).

## 4. THE 17-YEAR CYPRUS NON-DOMICILE (NON-DOM) SANCTUARY

Foreign nationals who take up tax residence in Cyprus and were not tax residents of Cyprus for at least 17 of the past 20 years qualify for the Non-Domicile regime upon registering with the Tax Department:

### 4.1 Fiscal Privileges for Proprietary Algorithmic Traders
1. **0% Capital Gains Tax on Trading:** Profits derived from the disposal of securities (equities, bonds, FX, exchange-traded options, futures, and financial derivatives) are **100% exempt from Cyprus income and capital gains tax**.
2. **0% Tax on Dividends:** Foreign or local dividend receipts paid into personal accounts are **100% exempt from the Special Defence Contribution (SDC)**.
3. **0% Tax on Interest:** Interest earned from bank deposits or sovereign bond coupon ladders (e.g. US Treasuries) is **100% exempt from SDC**.
4. **General Healthcare System (GeSY) Legal Cap:** Personal passive income (dividends, interest) is subject to a 2.65% GeSY healthcare contribution. Crucially, GeSY contributions are **legally capped at an annual income ceiling of €180,000**. Your absolute maximum health contribution is capped at **€4,770/year**, regardless of portfolio gains.
5. **No Mandatory Local Company:** Trading through a personal Interactive Brokers LLC account is completely legal. You trade as a natural person under 0% Non-Dom rules, avoiding corporate accounting fees, corporate auditing mandates, and corporate taxes.

### 4.2 Updated 2026 Tax Modernization Realities
* **Corporate Income Tax (CIT) Rate:** Cyprus increased standard corporate tax from 12.5% to **15% (effective 2026)** to comply with global minimum tax standards. Because Pavel trades personal capital under the individual Non-Dom regime, this corporate tax increase has zero impact on his trading operations.
* **Cryptocurrency Asset Taxation:** Cyprus enacted **Article 20E of the Income Tax Law (effective 2026)**, introducing a **flat 8% tax on profits derived from the disposal of crypto assets**, with ring-fenced losses. Traditional financial securities, stocks, and derivatives trading remain under the **0% capital gains exemption**.

### 4.3 Establishing Cyprus Tax Residency
Choose between two flexible legal standards:
* **The 183-Day Rule:** Reside in Cyprus for >183 days in a calendar year.
* **The 60-Day Rule:** Spend at least 60 days in Cyprus, spend less than 183 days in any other single nation, not be tax resident elsewhere, maintain a permanent residential home (your purchased property), and register business/economic ties.
* **Permanent Residence Maintenance:** Under Category 6.2 rules, there is **no 183-day stay requirement to keep your permanent residence**. The family is only legally required to visit Cyprus **once every 2 years for at least 1 day** to keep PR cards active indefinitely.
