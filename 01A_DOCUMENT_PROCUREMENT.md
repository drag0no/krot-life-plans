### 1.2 Apostilled Russian Vital Records (Civil Status)
* **Documents Required:**
  * Russian Marriage Certificate (original paper certificate).
  * Russian Birth Certificates for both Pavel and Anna.
* **Hague Apostille Mandate:** Under the Hague Apostille Convention, documents from Russia must bear a physical, embossed/stamped Hague Apostille (*Апостиль*) to be legally recognized in Brazil, Cyprus, and Portugal.
* **How & Where to Acquire:**
  * **Procedure:** Hague Apostilles on domestic Russian civil status certificates can only be affixed by Civil Registry Offices (ZAGS) or the Ministry of Justice in the Russian Federation.
  * **Execution Strategy:** Execute a Russian notarized Power of Attorney (POA / *Доверенность*) at the Russian Embassy in Singapore granting a trusted relative or specialized legal agent in Russia the authority to retrieve fresh duplicate certificates (*повторные свидетельства*) from ZAGS and submit them to the regional ZAGS/Minyust archive for physical Apostille stamping. Have the apostilled originals dispatched to Singapore via secure courier (DHL / CDEK).
  * **Translation:** Sworn translations into Portuguese (*tradutor juramentado*) must be done inside Brazil for Brazilian immigration. Certified English translations should be completed for Cyprus and the UK.

### 1.3 Russian General Notarial Power of Attorney (*Генеральная Доверенность*)

* **How & Where to Acquire:** Drafted and executed directly at the Consular Section of the Russian Embassy in Singapore. Set validity for 5 to 10 years with full powers of substitution (*с правом передоверия*).
