* **The Statutory Advantage:** Under Article 25 of the Portuguese Nationality Regulation (*Regulamento da Nacionalidade Portuguesa*), citizens of CPLP nations (including Brazil) have their knowledge of the Portuguese language **legally presumed by statute**. The A2 exam is **completely waived**.

### 4.2 Simultaneous Double Filing at Year 5 (2034)

### 4.3 Passport Issuance (2035)
