## 3. SETTLEMENT (ILR) IN 3 YEARS & CITIZENSHIP RULES (GOV.UK VERIFIED)

### 3.1 Fast-Track Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) at Year 3
Under GOV.UK Global Talent settlement guidance:
* **The 3-Year Qualifying Period:** Individuals endorsed under the **"Exceptional Talent"** criteria are statutorily eligible to apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (settlement) after completing **3 years of continuous residence** in the UK.
* **Continuous Residence Standard:** Maximum of **180 days absence** from the UK in any 12-month rolling period.

### 3.2 The Mandatory "Earned Money in the UK" Requirement (Critical Invariant)
To qualify for ILR on the Global Talent route, the applicant **must provide independent, verifiable evidence that they earned money in the UK in their field of expertise (digital technology)** during their period of permission:
* **What Counts:** Gross income generated from software engineering, technical architecture consulting, specialist contracting, director salary, or dividend distributions from an active UK technology company.
* **What Does NOT Count:** Realized capital gains from trading personal brokerage accounts or passive investment returns.
* **Operational Execution:** Pavel should engage in high-value software architecture consulting or bill algorithmic software licensing fees through a UK corporate vehicle to establish an unchallengeable audit trail of UK tech earnings (e.g. £30k–£50k+/year) with HMRC.

### 3.3 Naturalisation as a British Citizen (Year 4/5)
Under Section 6(1) of the British Nationality Act 1981:
* **Timing:** Accessible **12 months after being granted ILR** (subject to completing a total of 5 continuous years of lawful UK residence).
* **Physical Absence Limits:**
  * Maximum **450 days outside the UK** across the entire 5-year qualifying period.
  * Maximum **90 days outside the UK** in the 12 months immediately preceding the application.
* **Integration Requirements:** Pass the standardized *Life in the UK Test* and demonstrate English language proficiency (CEFR Level B1, met via Pavel's university degrees).
* **Passport Output:** Biometric British Passports granting unconditional right of abode across the United Kingdom and the Common Travel Area (UK, Ireland, Isle of Man, Channel Islands).

## 5. TAXATION UNDER THE 4-YEAR FIG REGIME (EFFECTIVE APRIL 2025)

Effective 6 April 2025, the UK Government abolished the outdated remittance basis of taxation and enacted the modern **4-Year Foreign Income and Gains (FIG) Regime**:

### 5.1 The 4-Year Tax Holiday on Foreign Assets
* **Eligibility:** Individuals who become UK tax resident after having been non-UK tax resident for at least 10 consecutive tax years prior to arrival.
* **Tax Privilege:** For the first **4 tax years** of UK tax residence, qualifying foreign income and foreign capital gains are **100% exempt from UK tax**.
* **Remittance Freedom:** Unlike the old non-dom regime, funds qualifying under the FIG regime can be **brought into the UK without triggering any UK tax charge**.
* **Application to Pavel:** Foreign capital gains generated from offshore brokerage accounts (or offshore corporate dividends) can be remitted freely into UK bank accounts to fund family living expenses with 0% UK tax during Years 1 through 4.

### 5.2 Year 5+ Fiscal Reality & Trading Structuring
* Beginning in Year 5, worldwide income and gains become fully taxable under standard UK rules:
  * **Capital Gains Tax (CGT):** 18% (basic rate) / **24% (higher rate)** on securities and residential property.
  * **Income Tax:** 20% / 40% / **45% (additional rate over £125,140)**.
* **Badges of Trade Defense:** High-frequency, high-turnover algorithmic trading physically executed while in the UK risks being classified by HMRC as a financial trade (subject to 45% Income Tax + Class 4 National Insurance) rather than investment capital gains (24%). Pavel should maintain servers and automated trading infrastructure offshore or trade through structured investment wrappers to safeguard investment status.
